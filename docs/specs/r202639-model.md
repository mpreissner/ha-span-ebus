# PR 4a: r202639 model layer and local entities

**Status:** draft, 2026-11-12. Architect spec for `feature/r202639-model` (PR 4a). Local only: do not
commit this file, push it, or link to it.
**Parent:** `docs/specs/local-transport.md` (the master spec; its sections are cited as §n).
**Builds on:** `docs/specs/transport-manager.md` (PR 3).
**Grounded in:** `tests/fixtures/r202639/` (MAIN 40, firmware `spanos3/r202639/04`, 32 devices:
`distribution-enclosure` root, `lugs-01` UPSTREAM, `lugs-02` DOWNSTREAM, `circuit-01`..`circuit-29`).
Every property id below was read from `capture.json` `$description`s. Where the master spec names
something the fixture does not contain, this spec says so and nothing is invented.

## 0. Scope

**In 4a**
- `span_client/local/model.py`: pure, HA-free. It turns a `homie.DeviceTree` into panel, lugs, circuit
  and power-flow facts using the §6 semantics.
- Snapshot debounce on `SpanTransportManager`. The interval comes from an option (0–15 s) and defaults
  to 1 s.
- Local entities on the **sensor**, **switch** and **select** platforms:
  - panel sensors and diagnostic sensors;
  - power flows;
  - circuit power, current and energy (from the panel's own registers) and breaker rating, with the §9
    attribute set;
  - the circuit relay switch and the priority select, both commanding via `/set`.
- Availability from `$state` and from the transport, including the cloud-fallback rules in §3.
- Persisting the last tree, so restarts with the panel unreachable still show the entities and energy.

**Not in 4a**
- **PR 4b:**
  - BESS, MID, EVSE and Solar subdevices (no such devices exist in the fixture);
  - the binary_sensor, number, button (GFE) and lock platforms;
  - therefore also the door, ethernet, wifi and PCS-active entities, which are binary sensors (§3.3);
  - shed forecast (the `shed`, `shed-forecast` and `doe` capabilities are in `schema.json` but not
    described on this panel).
- **PR 5:** link migration, the circuit map, and feeding cloud readings into local entities.
- **PR 6:** safety options, command outcomes beyond "sent / not acknowledged", the command record and
  event.
- **Not in any PR here:** a diagnostics download platform (none exists today), and
  `meter/shared-with-*` groups (Tier 3).

### 1.1 `span_client/local/model.py`

The module imports only `homie`, `health.Transport`, the stdlib and `dataclasses`, never
`homeassistant`. All dataclasses are `frozen=True, slots=True`. A field is `None` when the panel has not
published the value, or the value failed to decode. **Nothing defaults to 0.**

`build_snapshot` rules:
- Only described devices count. A device whose `$description` has not arrived is skipped.
- **Classification:**
  - the root is `ROOT_CLASS`, children are `LUGS_CLASS` or `CIRCUIT_CLASS`;
  - any other class (bess, pv, evse, mid in 4b) becomes `Unmapped(device_class, None)`;
  - a property on a mapped device that the model does not read becomes `Unmapped(cls, key)`. The PII
    keys in §7 are read by nobody and are not listed.
- **Values:**
  - values come from `tree.value()`, which decodes;
  - an undecodable value is `None` (homie already logs it at debug);
  - `info/spaces` that does not parse as comma-separated ints gives `spaces=None` rather than a
    partial tuple.

### 2.2 Circuit sign and energy

| Role | `power` | consumed (kWh) | produced (kWh) | net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOAD (default; LOADS, SUBPANEL, MIXED, UNUSED, or no `feeds-role`) | `-raw` | `exported/1000` | `imported/1000` | consumed − produced |
| SOLAR, GENERATOR | `raw` | `exported/1000` | `imported/1000` | produced − consumed |
| STORAGE (4a; 4b re-homes it to the BESS device) | `-raw` | as LOAD | as LOAD | as LOAD |
| Remote CT (§2.5) | `raw` (+ = import, §6) | `exported/1000` | `imported/1000` | consumed − produced |

Consumed and produced are physical register names. They never swap by role; only the sign of `power`
and `net` follows the role, as §6 asks ("power and net energy are inverted"). The solar rows cannot be
verified from the capture, because no circuit publishes `connection/feeds-role` (Q5).

`-0.0` is normalised to `0.0`, so that a negated zero does not show as "-0".

### 2.3 Grid power-flow sign

The panel publishes `power-flows/grid = -1537.9` while it is importing (lugs +1264.6, site +1696.3).
The magnitudes differ because the values were captured at different moments.

**Proposed:** normalise grid to **+ = import**, which HA's energy dashboard and the lugs both use. The
model holds this sign in one constant, `GRID_FLOW_SIGN = -1`.

This is **blocking for merge**. Changing a sign after release corrupts the long-term statistics, so the
Coder must confirm it live (§6.2) before the PR leaves draft. `site`, `pv` and `battery` are passed
through raw. Battery is "+ = discharging", stated in the README in 4b; it is not published here.

### 2.4 Derived circuit facts

- `always_on = not relay_controllable`, and is `None` if `relay_controllable` is `None`.
- `sheddable`:
  - `None` if `priority` is `None` or `"UNKNOWN"`, or if `relay_controllable` is `None`;
  - otherwise `relay_controllable and priority != "NEVER"`.
- `has_switch = relay_controllable is True and relay_settable`.
- `has_priority_select = priority_settable`.

`relay_settable` and `priority_settable` come from the `$description` (`PropertyDef.settable`), not from
a value.

### 2.5 Remote CT (synthetic only)

A circuit is a remote CT when:
- its description has no `switch/relay` property; and
- either `info/spaces` is undeclared, or its published value is empty (`""` or the Homie NUL empty
  string).

The fixture has none. A remote CT:
- has no switch and no select;
- has power from `raw`, as in §2.2;
- keeps its circuit-style unique ids;
- lives on the panel device in 4a. The "Remote CT n" device (§6) comes with the 4b subdevice work, and
  moving an entity between devices later does not change its unique id.

## 3. Entity mapping

### 3.1 Conventions for every row

- **Panel key:** `K` is `transport.panel_key`, which is `CONF_LOCAL_SERIAL` in 4a.
  - `C` is the circuit's Homie device id, `L` is the lugs' device id, and `R` is the root prefix
    `K_distribution-enclosure/`.
- **Device:** every 4a entity is on the panel device. It is the same device as the Transport sensor:
  - `identifiers={(DOMAIN, local_serial)}`, `name=entry.title`, `manufacturer="SPAN"`;
  - `model` is `info/model` with `_` replaced by a space;
  - `sw_version` is `info/firmware-version` and `hw_version` is `info/hardware-version`;
  - `serial_number` is the local serial, held in the device registry only and never logged.
- **Naming:** `has_entity_name=True`.
  - Panel-level entities use a `translation_key`.
  - Circuit entities use `name=f"{circuit name} {suffix}"`, like the cloud entities (`"Oven power"`,
    `"Oven breaker"`). The name is read from the snapshot on every write, so a rename in the SPAN app
    shows up.
  - A circuit with no `info/name` uses `f"Circuit {spaces}"`, e.g. `"Circuit 9/11"`.
- **Units:**
  - power is W (`UnitOfPower.WATT`);
  - current is A, voltage is V and frequency is Hz;
  - energy is **kWh** (`Wh / 1000`), the same unit as the cloud `*_energy` sensors that PR 5 re-points,
    so the statistics unit never changes. `suggested_display_precision=3` (`ENERGY_PRECISION`).
- **Kind** is the `ValueKind` from §1.1, which drives availability (§4.4).
- **Created when:** "described" means the property is in the device's `$description`. "Published" means
  the property has carried a value at least once (sticky for the life of the entry: `PowerFlows.published`
  and the Store). Entities are never created for a property that is only in `schema.json`.

### 3.4 Collision analysis and PR 5 re-pointing

- **Cloud unique ids** are `f"{cloud serial}_{node}/{prop}"` and `f"{cloud serial}_{node}/{prop}_energy"`.
  The node is `circuit-<n>`, `site`, `panel` and so on; examples are `…_circuit-12/power`,
  `…_circuit-12/power_energy`, `…_circuit-12/relay`, `…_panel/overhead`, `…_panel/overhead_energy` and
  `…_transport`.
- **Local unique ids** always have one of these shapes:
  - `K_distribution-enclosure/…`
  - `K_lugs/<id>/…`
  - `K_circuit/<id>/<cap>/<prop>`

  The node segment is always followed by `/` and an id, never `circuit-<n>/`. No local id ends in
  `_energy`: energy ids end in `/meter/<x>-energy` or `/derived/net-energy`. So even if the cloud and
  local serials turn out equal (master Q1), the two sets **cannot collide**. A unit test asserts this
  over the fixture: no local uid matches `^[^_]+_[a-z]+(-\d+)?/[a-z_]+(_energy)?$`.
- **Device sharing.** If the serials *are* equal, the cloud and local entities of a local-first entry
  share one device during fallback. Names such as "Oven power" then repeat, and HA suffixes the
  entity_id (`_2`) of whichever registers second. Q7 covers this; PR 5 removes it.

**How PR 5 re-points.** There are three cases.
1. **Local-only entry, and a local-first entry created through the local flow:** `K` is the local
   serial, and PR 5 never re-keys these ids. PR 5 adds the circuit map, so `CloudTransport` feeds
   fallback readings into these same entities through the `fallback_has_value` seam (§4.4). It also
   raises a repair listing the cloud-keyed entities this PR's fallback created, for the user to delete.
2. **Upgraded cloud entry ("Link local panels", §4):** `K` is the existing cloud key. PR 5 sets
   `transport.panel_key` to the cloud key before the platforms set up. 4a never creates local entities in
   a cloud entry, so no 4a id is in the way. Legacy ids are rewritten as below:

| Legacy cloud uid | Canonical (4a shape, `K` = cloud key) |
|---|---|
| `K_circuit-<n>/power` | `K_circuit/C/meter/active-power` |
| `K_circuit-<n>/power_energy` | `K_circuit/C/meter/exported-energy` (consumed; the one-time statistics step from §3) |
| `K_circuit-<n>/relay` | `K_circuit/C/switch/relay` |
| `K_panel/overhead`, `…_energy`, site flows replaced by `power-flows/*` | not re-pointed; restored as unavailable and listed in the legacy repair (§5) |

   `n → C` comes from the link record's `circuits` map, which matches on spaces.
3. **Identity helpers:** all ids are built by `model.panel_uid` / `lugs_uid` / `circuit_uid`, so PR 5's
   migration produces the same strings that 4a creates.

## 4. Runtime wiring

### 4.1 Snapshot debounce

The debounce lives in `SpanTransportManager`:

```python
self._interval = float(entry.options.get(CONF_SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL, DEFAULT_SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL))  # 0..15
self._dirty = False
self._flush_handle: CALLBACK_TYPE | asyncio.Handle | None = None

@callback
def _on_message(self, topic, payload, retained):
    ...existing tree.apply + health...
    if parsed is not None:
        self._mark_dirty()

@callback
def _mark_dirty(self) -> None:
    self._dirty = True
    if self._flush_handle is not None or self._stopped:
        return
    if self._interval <= 0:
        self._flush_handle = self._hass.loop.call_soon(self._flush)
    else:
        self._flush_handle = async_call_later(self._hass, self._interval, self._flush)

@callback
def _flush(self, _now: object = None) -> None:
    self._flush_handle = None
    if not self._dirty or self._stopped:
        return
    self._dirty = False
    self.snapshot = model.build_snapshot(self.tree, self._root())
    self._log_unmapped(self.snapshot.unmapped)
    self._store_tree_later()
    for listener in list(self._model_listeners):
        listener(self.snapshot)
```

- **Trailing window.** The first message after a flush schedules the next flush `interval` seconds
  later, and everything in between is coalesced. Latency is at most `interval`; dispatch rate is at most
  1/`interval`.
- **Cost.** `build_snapshot` is a pure walk over about 32 devices. It runs on the event loop and needs no
  executor.
- **Refresh on transport change.** `_refresh_shown` also calls `_mark_dirty()`, so presence re-evaluates
  on a transport change. Entities additionally listen to `async_add_listener`, so either path is enough.
- **Stopping.** `async_stop` cancels `_flush_handle`, clears model listeners, and saves the Store once.
- **Interval UI:** see Q9. The recommendation is to read the option with its default but ship no UI in
  4a.

### 4.2 Platforms and entity creation

`runtime.transport` is the attachment point; `SpanRuntime` needs no change. In each platform's
`async_setup_entry`:

```python
if (transport := runtime.transport) is not None:
    async_add_entities([SpanTransportSensor(entry, transport)])   # sensor only, unchanged
    local_entity.async_setup_local(entry, transport, async_add_entities, PLATFORM_DESCRIPTIONS)
runtime.add_platform(lambda c: _register(c, async_add_entities))  # cloud path unchanged
```

`async_setup_local`:
- keeps a `known: set[str]` of unique ids;
- registers a model listener that, for each snapshot, walks the platform's descriptions;
- creates the entities whose `exists_fn(snapshot, …)` is true and whose uid is not yet known;
- calls `async_add_entities(new)` once per snapshot;
- also runs once right away against `transport.snapshot` (which may be the restored one, §5);
- removes its listener with `entry.async_on_unload`.

**Entity descriptions** live in `local_entity.py`. They are frozen dataclasses extending
`SensorEntityDescription` / `SwitchEntityDescription` / `SelectEntityDescription` with:
- `scope`: PANEL, UPSTREAM, DOWNSTREAM or CIRCUIT;
- `uid_fn(panel_key, device_id) -> str`;
- `exists_fn(snapshot, device_id) -> bool`;
- `value_fn(snapshot, device_id) -> Any`;
- `kind: ValueKind`;
- `attrs_fn` (optional).

The §3 tables are these descriptions, one row each.

**`SpanLocalEntity` base:**
- `_attr_should_poll=False` and `has_entity_name=True`;
- holds `transport`, `device_id` and its description;
- on add, subscribes to the model listener and the transport listener;
- computes `(presence, value, attrs)`, and calls `async_write_ha_state()` **only if that tuple changed**,
  which avoids pointless writes at 1 Hz;
- `available` is `presence is not UNAVAILABLE`;
- `native_value` is `None` for UNKNOWN.

**Vanished devices and properties.** A circuit or property that disappears from the description, for
example after a firmware update, makes `exists_fn` false, and the entity becomes unavailable. It is never
removed automatically; a repair for that is out of scope.

### 4.3 Unknown properties and classes

- `Snapshot.unmapped` lists them. The manager logs **once per (class, key) per entry run**, at debug:
  `"r202639 model: ignoring %s property %s"` (class and key only, never a device id or serial).
- 4a creates **no generic entities** for them. Q8 covers this; the master's "adoption" is later work.
- Unmapped device classes (bess, pv, evse, mid) are logged the same way, once, as
  `"… %s devices are not modelled yet"`.
- Enum values outside the declared `format` decode to `None` and show as unknown.

### 4.4 Availability and the cloud fallback

`model.presence(kind, shown, device_state, has_value, fallback_has_value)`:

| Kind | `shown`=LOCAL and device up¹ | LOCAL, device not up | `shown`=CLOUD | `shown`=OFFLINE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MEASUREMENT | VALUE (None → unknown, never 0) | UNAVAILABLE | **UNKNOWN** (4a); PR 5: VALUE if `fallback_has_value` else UNKNOWN | UNAVAILABLE |
| STATIC | VALUE | VALUE if `has_value` else UNAVAILABLE | VALUE if `has_value` else UNKNOWN | UNAVAILABLE |
| ENERGY | VALUE if `has_value` else UNAVAILABLE | same | same (holds last value) | same |
| CONTROL | VALUE if `has_value` else UNAVAILABLE | UNAVAILABLE | UNAVAILABLE (4a; PR 5 may carry the relay) | UNAVAILABLE |

¹ The device is up when `tree.effective_state(device_id) in UP_STATES`. For panel-scope entities that is
the root; for lugs and circuits it is their effective state, so a root that is not ready overrides them.

- **Energy** is available whenever the tree holds a value, live or restored (§5), in every transport
  state. This is what the brief requires: energy holds and stays available. On failback the registers
  resume, and `total_increasing` sees the real delta.
- **Local-only entry** (`Mode.LOCAL_ONLY`, or no cloud credentials):
  - `shown` is only ever LOCAL or OFFLINE;
  - losing the link makes everything unavailable except energy (held), which matches §2.2;
  - nothing is stubbed.
- **Local-first entry in 4a:**
  - **On CLOUD:** local measurements go unknown, controls go unavailable, and energy and static values
    hold.
  - **PR 3's cloud entities** still appear on failover, keyed on the cloud serial, exactly as today.
    That remains the only way to see live data during fallback until PR 5 (Q7).
  - **The PR 5 seam:** `local_entity` calls `transport.fallback_value(scope, device_id, key)`. In 4a this
    returns the module sentinel `NO_FALLBACK`, so `fallback_has_value` is always False. PR 5 implements
    it from the link map and the cloud coordinator's data.

### 4.5 Command path

```python
async def async_send_command(self, device_id: str, key: str, value: Any) -> PublishResult:
    if self.shown is not Transport.LOCAL:
        raise HomeAssistantError(translation_domain=DOMAIN, translation_key="local_unavailable")
    description = self.tree.descriptions.get(device_id)
    definition = description.properties.get(key) if description else None
    if definition is None or not definition.settable:
        raise HomeAssistantError(translation_domain=DOMAIN, translation_key="not_settable")
    try:
        payload = homie.encode_value(definition, value)
    except ValueError as err:
        raise ServiceValidationError(translation_domain=DOMAIN, translation_key="bad_value") from err
    try:
        await self._mqtt.publish(homie.set_topic(device_id, key), payload, qos=1)
    except CannotConnect as err:
        raise HomeAssistantError(translation_domain=DOMAIN, translation_key="command_not_sent") from err
    except OutcomeUnknown:
        _LOGGER.warning("SPAN panel did not acknowledge a %s command", key)   # key only
        return PublishResult.UNACKED
    return PublishResult.ACKED
```

- **No topic, device id or serial** appears in an exception message or a log above debug; the topic
  contains the device id. `from err` keeps the chain for debug tracebacks. The `mqtt.py` exception texts
  carry no topic: "not connected", "publish refused: <rc>".
- **Nothing is retried.** That matches `mqtt.py`'s "a stale command is worse than a lost one".
- **Safety options and the outcome event** are PR 6, which adds them inside this method (§8.2 says they
  are enforced here).

**Optimistic versus confirmed state (switch and select)**
- On a successful call, whether ACKED or UNACKED, the entity sets
  `self._pending = (value, monotonic() + CONFIRM_WINDOW)` with `CONFIRM_WINDOW = 10.0` (§8.3), and
  writes the pending value straight away.
- On each snapshot:
  - if the tree value equals the pending value, the state is **confirmed**: clear `_pending`;
  - if the deadline has passed, clear `_pending` and show the tree value, and log at debug
    "not confirmed within 10 s" (key only);
  - otherwise keep showing the pending value.
- **A deadline timer** (`async_call_later(CONFIRM_WINDOW)`) also re-evaluates, so the state reverts even
  if no snapshot arrives.
- **Errors** (HomeAssistantError or ServiceValidationError) leave no pending value and change no state.
- **Presence still wins.** If the entity becomes UNAVAILABLE, the pending value is dropped.
- **The switch:**
  - `turn_on` sends `"CLOSED"` and `turn_off` sends `"OPEN"`;
  - `is_on` is True for CLOSED, False for OPEN, and None otherwise, the same rule as the cloud switch.
- **The select:**
  - `select_option(option)` maps the lower-case option back to the enum value;
  - `UNKNOWN` is never offered.

### 4.6 Device registry

The panel device is created by the first local entity, or by the Transport sensor; both pass the same
`identifiers`. `model`, `sw_version` and `hw_version` come from `PanelFacts`. When they change (a
firmware update), `local_entity` calls `device_registry.async_update_device` once per change, not on
every snapshot.

## 5. Restoring last values across restarts

There are two layers.

1. **Tree Store** (§6: "the schema cache plus the last values kept in Store")
   - **Store:** `Store(hass, 1, f"{DOMAIN}.{entry_id}.local_tree")`, holding:
     - `descriptions`: `{device_id: raw $description JSON}`;
     - `values`: `{device_id: {key: payload text}}`.

     It holds **no `$state`**: restored devices start UNKNOWN.
   - **Loading:** `async_start` loads the Store **before** connecting. It replays each item through
     `tree.apply()` using synthetic topics built by `homie` (a new helper, `homie.value_topic(device_id,
     key)` and `homie.description_topic(device_id)`), then builds `snapshot` with `restored=True` and
     dispatches it. The platforms therefore create every entity at startup even with the panel
     unreachable:
     - energy shows its last register value;
     - everything else is unavailable or unknown per §4.4, because the root state is UNKNOWN.
   - **Live values replace restored ones.** On connect the broker resends all retained values (every
     r202639 property is `retained=true`), overwriting the restored ones.
   - **Saving:** `async_delay_save(…, 300)` after any flush whose snapshot differs, and once in
     `async_stop`. That is at most one write every 5 minutes.
   - **Excluded from the Store:** the PII keys in §7 and `status/wifi-ssid`. The Store is in `.storage`,
     not in logs or fixtures, but there is no reason to keep data no entity reads.
   - **Removal:** `async_remove_entry` must delete this Store explicitly with
     `Store(...).async_remove()`, because the current prefix glob only matches `*.energy`. Unload keeps
     it.
   - **Version mismatch or corrupt data:** log at debug and start empty. An empty start is always safe.
2. **RestoreSensor on energy entities only:**
   - this is a safety net if the Store is lost;
   - if the tree has no value, the entity shows the restored native value;
   - it never shows 0 when there is no value, and it is available iff it has a value.

   Measurements are **not** restored: a stale watt figure after a restart is wrong data.

## 7. Privacy

- **Never read into facts, entities, attributes, the Store or logs:**
  - root `info/name`, `info/address-lines`, `info/latitude`, `info/longitude`, `info/locality`,
    `info/region`, `info/serial-number`, `info/utility-meter-serial-number` and `info/country-code`;
  - `status/postal-code` and `status/wifi-ssid`.

  `model.PII_KEYS` lists them. The model skips them and does not list them as unmapped, and
  `store_payload` drops them. The Wi-Fi SSID attribute (§9) is therefore **not** shipped; Q10 asks
  whether to keep it out for good.
- **Logs:**
  - property keys and device classes may be logged;
  - device ids, topics, the serial, the host and circuit names are logged only at debug, and only where
    unavoidable;
  - exceptions raised to the user use translation keys, never interpolated ids.
- **Fixtures:** the new synthetic tests use `nt-0000-test` and `circuit-NN` ids. Nothing new is
  captured from the live panel for this PR. If the Coder regenerates the capture, it goes through
  `tools/probe_local.py`'s redaction, and `test_probe_redaction.py` must pass.
- **Unique ids and the device registry** hold the local serial. That is unavoidable, as it is today for
  the cloud serial, and they are not logs.
- **This spec** quotes only redacted fixture values.

## 8. Open questions (with recommendations)

1. **Grid flow sign.** `power-flows/grid` is −1537.9 while the lugs import +1264.6.
   - **Recommend:** normalise to + = import (`GRID_FLOW_SIGN = -1`), and confirm live before merge
     (§6.2 step 2).
2. **"Never-backup = priority not settable" (§6) does not match the data.**
   - In the fixture, not-settable coincides with `relay-controllable=false` on both the relay and the
     priority, while NEVER-priority circuits stay settable.
   - **Recommend:**
     - create the select iff `settable`, which §6 already says;
     - **do not** derive a "never backup" fact from `settable`;
     - correct the §6 wording in the master spec.
3. **Recorder growth.** Per-second power across 29 circuits plus panel values.
   - **Recommend:** round native values in the entity layer: W to an integer, A to 0.1, V to 0.1, Hz to
     0.01. Keep the 1 s interval, which the user wants for power. Rely on write-on-change (§4.2). Put the
     rounding behind one constant so the user's pending decision is a one-line change.
   - Needs the user's sign-off; it is their open top-priority decision.
4. **Circuit current enabled by default?**
   - **Recommend:** disabled. It is 29 more per-second series, and power plus nominal voltage covers
     most needs.
5. **Solar role untestable.** No circuit publishes `connection/feeds-role`.
   - **Recommend:** ship the §2.2 rule with synthetic tests. Ask a contributor with PV (on their PR
     thread, per the contributor memory) for a capture before 4b's Solar device.
6. **Feedthrough figures.** `lugs-02` publishes currents only, and the sign of its power and energy is
   unknown.
   - **Recommend:** current entities disabled by default; power and energy created only once published,
     disabled, raw sign. Revisit the sign when someone with a feedthrough load publishes it.
7. **Duplicate entities during fallback for local-first entries in 4a.** PR 3's cloud entities appear on
   failover beside the now-unknown local ones.
   - **Recommend:** keep PR 3's behaviour until PR 5. It is the only live data during fallback, and
     suppressing it would be a regression. PR 5's link map then feeds the local entities, and a repair
     retires the cloud-keyed ones.
8. **Generic entities for unknown properties.**
   - **Recommend:** none in 4a; debug log once. Adoption is a later, deliberate feature, because generic
     per-second sensors would worsen recorder growth.
9. **Snapshot interval UI.** A local-only entry has no options flow today (`async_step_init` aborts
   `not_cloud_account`), so adding the field means a new step for local-only plus a field on `transport`.
   - **Recommend:** **defer the UI.** Read `CONF_SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL` with its default (1 s, clamped to
     0–15), so the UI is a small follow-up, or part of PR 6's options work.
10. **Wi-Fi SSID attribute (§9).**
    - **Recommend:** leave it out permanently. It identifies the household network and adds nothing to
      energy management.
11. **Upstream service rating versus protection rating.** §9 lists "upstream protection rating", but
    only `connection/service-rating` is published.
    - **Recommend:** ship both as separate diagnostics; overcurrent-protection is created only once
      published. Do not alias one to the other.
12. **Circuit energy naming.** The cloud calls it "<name> energy"; this spec says "consumed energy".
    - **Recommend:** "consumed energy" for new local entities. For PR 5's re-pointed entities, HA keeps
      the user's entity_id and friendly-name overrides, so existing dashboards are unaffected.
13. **Is the panel `breaker/rating` the main breaker, or the bus rating?** The fixture shows 200 A with
    an upstream service rating of 200 A.
    - **Recommend:** name it "Main breaker rating" (as in §9), and check it against the physical panel
      during the live test.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-12)

- **Q1 grid sign:** flip it so positive means import (`GRID_FLOW_SIGN = -1`). Confirm on the live panel before merge.
- **Q3 recorder growth:** round values and write only on change.
  - Precision: W to whole numbers, A and V to 0.1, Hz to 0.01.
  - Keep the 1 s snapshot interval.
  - An entity writes state only when its rounded value, or its availability, changes.
